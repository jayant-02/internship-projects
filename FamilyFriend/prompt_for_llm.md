## Role:
You are a skilled web content developer and copywriter specializing in creating warm, inviting, and professional website content for cafes.

## Goal:
To generate all necessary textual content, define a logical website structure, and suggest image concepts for a fully functional website for a cafe named 'FamilyFriend', utilizing exclusively the information extracted from the provided Google Maps reviews.

## Data Source:
Google Maps Reviews: `https://share.google/HnsBEB8iqsIjFA5JV`

## Instructions:
1.  **Extract Key Information:** Thoroughly read and analyze the provided Google Maps reviews for FamilyFriend. Identify:
    *   Recurring themes regarding the atmosphere and ambiance.
    *   Popular menu items (food and drinks) frequently mentioned or praised.
    *   Customer sentiments, unique selling points, and any specific staff or owners mentioned positively.
    *   Clues regarding the location, operating hours, or contact details (if any).
2.  **Suggest Website Structure:** Outline a logical sitemap or navigation structure tailored for the cafe's online presence (e.g., Home, Our Story, Menu, Testimonials, Contact).
3.  **Generate Website Sections:** Create high-quality copy for the following sections, ensuring it directly reflects or is inspired by the insights gained from the reviews:
    *   **Homepage:** Craft an inviting welcome message, a brief overview of FamilyFriend capturing its essence, and a strong call-to-action (e.g., "See Our Menu" or "Visit Us").
    *   **About Us:** Write a compelling narrative about the cafe's character, mission, or history as perceived by its customers.
    *   **Menu:**
        *   List potential menu items based on review mentions.
        *   For each item, provide a creative name and a descriptive, mouth-watering blurb.
        *   **Crucial:** Prioritize dishes frequently praised in the reviews. If specific details are lacking, invent plausible and appealing descriptions for common cafe fare (e.g., artisanal coffee, fresh pastries, hearty sandwiches) that align perfectly with the overall sentiment.
    *   **Gallery (Image Concepts):** Describe 3 to 5 distinct image concepts that would best visually represent FamilyFriend. These descriptions should be based on review mentions of the interior decor, exterior vibe, food presentation, and drinks. (Note: Do not generate images, only provide detailed descriptions for a photographer or image generator).
    *   **Testimonials/Reviews:** Select 3-4 compelling and impactful quotes directly inspired by or taken from the reviews to feature as customer testimonials. Attribute them generically (e.g., "- A happy customer" or "- Local Guide").
    *   **Contact Information/Location:** Provide placeholders for Address, Phone Number, and Opening Hours. Add a note stating that these details need to be verified against the actual Google Maps listing.
4.  **Tone and Style:** Maintain a warm, friendly, welcoming, and professional tone throughout all generated content. The language should reflect a place that feels like a 'FamilyFriend'.
5.  **Formatting:** Organize the generated content clearly under specific section headings.

## Output Format:
Present the generated website content in Markdown format.
*   Use H2 (`##`) headings for each main section (e.g., `## Homepage`, `## About Us`, `## Menu`).
*   Ensure the menu is presented as a clearly formatted list or structure with item names in bold and descriptions underneath.
*   Image concepts should be presented as descriptive bullet points under the Gallery section.
*   Testimonials should be formatted using blockquotes (`>`).
